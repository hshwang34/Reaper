SETUP GUIDE

From download to your first hijack in about 10 minutes.

The app walks you through each step, and does most of it for you. Here’s what to expect, with the real screens.

0 of 9 steps done

  1. Download the app
  2. Sign in with Twitch
  3. Connect OBS
  4. Pick your camera
  5. Set up payouts
  6. Read the safety basics
  7. Run a free test
  8. Share your hijack page
  9. Set your rules

STEP 1 · 2 min

Download the app

Stream Hijack is a small app that sits next to OBS. Join the alpha and we’ll email you the download.

  • Open Stream-Hijack-mac.dmg and drag Stream Hijack into Applications. One download works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  • Run Stream-Hijack-Setup.exe. It installs just for you, so it won’t ask for an admin password.
Mac says it “could not verify” the app? That’s normal during the alpha. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
Windows says it “protected your PC”? That’s normal during the alpha. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Join the alpha to get it

You’ll need
  • macOS 13 or newerWindows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • OBS Studio 30 or newer
  • A Twitch account
  • A webcam
  • About 10 minutes

STEP 2 · 1 min

Sign in with Twitch

Open Stream Hijack and click Sign in with Twitch. Your browser opens; approve it there and come back.

That’s it. Your hijack page, payments and the AI are all handled for you. Nothing to install or paste.

Not working?

If the app still says “Waiting for your browser…” after a minute, click the button again. Make sure you finished signing in on the Twitch page.

STEP 3 · 2 min

Connect OBS

The app finds OBS for you. If OBS needs permission to be controlled, the app asks you to close OBS and clicks Switch it on for you.

Then click Add the overlay. The app adds one hidden AI Hijack overlay to your scenes. It only shows while a hijack is playing. Nothing else in your scenes changes.

Not working?
  • “OBS is still open”: close OBS completely (check the menu barsystem tray), then try again.
  • Never connects: update OBS to version 30 or newer. You can also turn it on yourself in OBS under “Tools → WebSocket Server Settings → Enable WebSocket server”.
  • Overlay in the wrong place: in OBS, drag AI Hijack to the top of your Sources, above your camera.

STEP 4 · 2 min

Pick your camera

Stream Hijack uses OBS’s Virtual Camera, so effects cover exactly what chat sees: your framing, filters and all.

  1. In OBS, click the gear next to Start Virtual Camera and set the output type to Source, then pick your webcam. Not Program: that would show your stream inside itself.
  2. Click Start Virtual Camera in the app (or in OBS).
  3. When you see yourself and a green check, click Looks good, continue.
First time on a Mac? macOS asks you to allow the OBS Virtual Camera once, under System Settings → Privacy & Security. Allow camera access for Stream Hijack too.
Not working?
  • Black or frozen picture: make sure nothing else is using the camera, and that you allowed camera access.
  • “This is not the OBS Virtual Camera”: start the Virtual Camera in OBS, then pick it in the app.

STEP 5 · 3 min

Set up payouts

Chat pays by card on your hijack page, and Stripe sends your share to your bank. Click Set up payouts with Stripe. Stripe asks for your ID and bank details; we never see them.

Every dollar buys one second. Out of each payment, Stripe takes its card fee and we take the AI time plus 5%. You keep the rest.

STEP 6 · 1 min

Read the safety basics

Tick four boxes so you know how it works: you own what’s on your stream, you know the panic key, you tell viewers it’s AI, and photos of people need their consent.

Nudity, anyone who may be under 18, real public figures, hate, gore and self-harm are always blocked, whatever you choose later.

STEP 7 · 1 min

Run a free test

Click Run the 10-second test and watch OBS’s Program view, not the Preview.

  1. For 2–5 seconds nothing changes while the AI warms up.
  2. Then, for about 10 seconds, your camera turns into 80s anime on stream.
  3. Then it snaps back on its own. Nobody is charged.

You get one free test a day.

Not working?
  • The app showed the effect but OBS didn’t: the overlay is in another scene or under your camera. Click Add the overlay again, or drag AI Hijack to the top of your Sources.
  • Nothing happened at all: go back to Connect OBS and check it says connected.

STEP 8 · 1 min

Share your hijack page

Your hijack page is where chat picks an effect and pays. Copy the link from the Live tab and put it where chat will see it:

  • a !hijack chat command
  • a panel under your stream
  • your stream title, for example “hijacks are ON (link in chat)”

STEP 9 · 2 min

Set your rules

Open the Rules tab to choose what chat can do: Family-friendly, Balanced or Anything goes, then fine-tune custom effects, photos, how wild it gets, and the price and length. Changes save instantly.

Try the rules on our homepage

YOU’RE READY

Go live. Two things to remember.

Keep the app open.

Stream Hijack runs each hijack, so keep its window open while you stream. It can sit behind OBS.

Know your panic key.

Press ⌘⇧HCtrlShiftH from any app. The effect disappears at once and new hijacks wait in line until you press it again or click Resume hijacks.

Still stuck?

A real person will help. Email support@streamhijack.com or ask in our Discord. You can run setup again any time from the Stream Hijack icon in your menu barsystem tray: Run setup again…
