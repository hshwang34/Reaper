Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Install

    Open Stream-Hijack-mac.dmg and drag Stream Hijack into Applications.

  2. 2

    Sign in with Twitch

    Open the app and click Sign in with Twitch. Your hijack page, payments and the AI are handled for you.

  3. 3

    Follow the setup

    The app connects OBS, picks your camera, sets up payouts and runs a free test hijack. See every step with screenshots.

What you need

  • A Mac on macOS 13 Ventura or newer. One download runs on Apple silicon and Intel.
  • OBS Studio 30 or newer. The app adds its overlay for you.
  • A webcam and a Twitch account to sign in with. You can stream to Twitch, Kick or YouTube.
  • A Stripe account to get paid. The app sets one up with you in a few minutes.
