    BEFORE YOU HIT GO

    Good questions.
    Straight answers.

    Still curious? Ask us in Discord or email hello@streamhijack.com

    What do I need?

    OBS Studio with its WebSocket server on, a Stripe account to get paid (the app sets it up with you), and the Stream Hijack desktop app, for macOS or Windows.

    Is this a Twitch extension?

    No. Viewers pay by card on your own hijack page, so it works alongside Twitch, Kick or YouTube. No Bits, no extension review.

    Where does the money go?

    Viewers pay by card on your hijack page, through Stripe. Every dollar buys one second. Each payment first covers the AI video time it uses (2¢ a second), a 5% platform fee and Stripe's card fee; the rest goes to your Stripe account. On a $10 hijack you keep about $8.71. See the pricing breakdown. If a paid hijack never plays, the viewer is refunded automatically.

    How long does a hijack last?

    As many seconds as they paid for, up to the maximum you set. A short wipe covers the cut in and the cut out.

    Is there a delay?

    The restyled picture runs a little behind your camera, as any live AI video does. The wipe hides the jump at both ends, and the countdown is on screen so viewers know what is happening.

    What can a viewer make it do?

    An effect you have turned on, or their own idea if you allow typed prompts. Every typed idea and photo is screened against your rules before it runs, and nudity, minors and real people are always blocked.

    Who runs Stream Hijack?

    Stream Hijack is an independent product built by Hyunseok Hwang. You can reach a real person at support@streamhijack.com or in our Discord. The rules are in our Terms and Privacy Policy.

    Does it cost me anything?

    Nothing up front. Each viewer payment covers its own AI video time plus a small platform fee, so you never pay for a hijack yourself.
