Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 22 OCTOBER 2026

The short version

  • We never see or store card numbers. Stripe handles payments.
  • Uploaded photos are deleted when the hijack ends, within one hour at the latest.
  • We keep a record of each hijack (who, how much, how long, what was asked for) so streamers can see it and we can handle refunds and abuse.
  • We don't sell your data and this website has no ads or tracking cookies.

1. Who is responsible

Stream Hijack, built and run by Hyunseok Hwang, is responsible for the data described here. Contact: support@streamhijack.com.

2. What we collect

From viewers who pay for a hijack:

  • the name you enter, the effect you pick or the idea you type, and any photo you upload;
  • the amount, the length and the outcome of the hijack;
  • the results of the safety screening, and a salted, one-way hash of your IP address used to block abuse.

From streamers:

  • your Twitch account ID, login name and display name when you sign in with Twitch;
  • your Stripe account ID and whether payouts are set up (Stripe holds your banking and identity details, not us);
  • your settings and the record of hijacks on your channel.

Card details are entered on Stripe's payment page and go straight to Stripe. We never receive them.

This website (streamhijack.com) sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

3. How we use it

  • to screen and run the hijack you paid for, and show it on the streamer's camera;
  • to take payments, pay streamers, and refund hijacks that never played;
  • to show streamers their recent hijacks;
  • to prevent abuse, enforce our Terms, and meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run Stream Hijack, each for its part of the job:

  • Stripe: card payments, payouts and refunds.
  • Decart: the AI video model. It receives the streamer's camera feed, the effect or idea, and any photo, only while the hijack plays.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic and Sightengine: automatic safety screening of typed ideas and photos before anything runs.
  • Twitch: streamer sign-in.
  • Fly.io and Vercel: hosting for the service and this website.

The viewer name, effect and length appear on the streamer's live stream; that is the point of a hijack. We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. We disclose data to authorities when the law requires it, including reports of suspected child sexual abuse material.

5. How long we keep it

  • Uploaded photos: deleted when the hijack ends or expires, and within one hour at the latest. The one exception is a photo flagged as suspected child sexual abuse material, which the law requires us to preserve and report.
  • Hijack and payment records: kept while the streamer's account is active, and as long as needed for accounting, refunds and disputes.
  • Streamer accounts: deleted on request, except records we must keep by law.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to support@streamhijack.com. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or California law) you may have further rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Age

Stream Hijack is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will tell streamers in advance.
