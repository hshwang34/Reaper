2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old and able to make a binding agreement. By using the service you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. Stream Hijack is in early access (alpha): features may change, and parts of it may not work as described.

3. How payments work

  • Price. Every dollar buys one second of hijack, up to the maximum length the streamer sets. Streamers also set the lowest price.
  • Who you pay. Viewers pay the streamer by card through Stripe. The streamer is the seller; Stripe processes the card and pays the streamer out.
  • Fees. From each payment, Stream Hijack keeps the cost of the AI video time it uses (2¢ a second) plus a 5% platform fee, and Stripe deducts its card processing fee. The rest goes to the streamer. Streamers pay nothing up front and no monthly fee.
  • Refunds. If a paid hijack never plays (for example, the stream is offline, the streamer turns it down, or something fails before it starts), the payment is refunded to the viewer automatically, including our fee. Once a hijack has started playing it is not refunded, even if the streamer ends it early. If something went wrong after it started, write to support and we will look at it.
  • Chargebacks. Disputes go through Stripe. Filing a dispute for a hijack that played may get you blocked from the service.

4. What you may not ask for

Every typed idea and photo is screened before it can run, and anything that breaks these rules is refused. You may not submit, or try to get around screening to produce:

  • nudity or sexual content, or anything involving minors;
  • a real person's face or likeness, including celebrities, unless it is your own and you consent;
  • hate, harassment, threats, or content targeting someone for who they are;
  • graphic violence, self-harm, or anything illegal;
  • other people's trademarks or copyrighted characters in a way that misleads or infringes;
  • anything that breaks the rules of the platform the streamer is live on.

Suspected child sexual abuse material is never shown, is preserved, and is reported to the authorities as the law requires. Screening is automatic and not perfect; the streamer can end any hijack instantly.
